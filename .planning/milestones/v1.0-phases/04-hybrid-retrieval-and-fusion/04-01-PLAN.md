<objective>
Establish the decision-gated, production-quality tracer: a secret-safe query vector plus one dense and one lexical requester-RLS channel, rank-only RRF, and retrieval meta. This is deliberately evidence retrieval only.
</objective>

## Flagged assumptions requiring manual confirmation

- RTV-01: exact wave sequencing and re-fusion edge conditions are not classified; this tracer proves one first-wave subset only and Plan 03 must prove all four plus graph.
- RTV-02: policy limit boundaries, rank/score precision, and tie-breaking are contracts below: positive integer limits, 1-based rank, `weight/(k+rank)`, lexical canonical-ID order ties.
- RTV-03–RTV-09: no SPEC edge classification exists; their acceptance criteria in Plans 02–04 are the explicit provisional contract and require verification review before a default changes.
